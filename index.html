$ cat about.md

      

        
The bug I keep finding is the same one wearing different clothes: a system reports a state it is not in. A policy engine says pass after checking zero resources. A gateway marks a policy Accepted while Envoy rejects the backend. A rollback says Synced while the old revision runs.

        
Nobody files these, because nothing looks broken. The dashboard is green. So I read the status, then go and look at what actually happened -- the goroutine count, the Envoy config, the task set tag -- and write down the difference.

        
Every fix I send comes with the measurement that shows the gap. A claim I can't reproduce doesn't get posted.

        
Merged in Kyverno and KEDA, with fixes in review at PipeCD, kgateway, OpenKruise, KubeEdge and Volcano. Looking for internships.
